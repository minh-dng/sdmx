# Contributing

These guidelines apply to [minh-dng/sdmx](https://github.com/minh-dng/sdmx), an
independently maintained fork of [khaeru/sdmx](https://github.com/khaeru/sdmx).
Upstream has its own contribution policy; these guidelines describe this fork only.

Would-be contributors are kindly asked to:

- Read and understand the following Code of Conduct.
- Read the documentation, in particular:

  - The [index page](https://minh-dng.github.io/sdmx/)
    (doc/index.rst in the source distribution or Git repository)
    gives general information about project communications.
  - The [‘Development’ page](https://minh-dng.github.io/sdmx/dev.html)
    (doc/dev.rst)
    gives specific instructions for code changes.

- Open an issue or pull request at https://github.com/minh-dng/sdmx,
  or otherwise get in touch as indicated at the above locations.

# Code of Conduct

`sdmx1` is developed by and for a community
of members who act as maintainer(s), developer(s), and/or user(s).
Like all communities, this one involves mutual respect,
responsibility, and obligations between members.
These relations are based on shared values and principles,
and extend to people in other, broader communities to which members also belong.

Specifically,
this community aims to be **healthy**, **inclusive**, and **ethical**
in both the development and use of the software.

To this end, individual members:

- Practice empathy and kindness towards others;
- Appreciate others' labour and contributions of all kinds,
  and give due credit for it;
- Accept and seek to overcome personal difference; and
- Exhibit humility and grace;
  especially in receiving feedback about their actions.

Community members **do not**:

- Harass other members in any way;
- Attack or endanger others in any way,
  or create conditions that would prevent their participation
  in the community;
  
  - This includes actions based on any personal characteristic.
    Many lists of such characteristics exist.
    See references (1–2).

- Presume entitlement to or otherwise demand the labour of others;
- Misrepresent others' labour as their own,
  or reuse others' work without license or permission;
- Attempt to evade or undermine this Code of Conduct,
  or damage the project or community more generally.

To uphold this Code of Conduct, the
[maintainers of this fork](https://github.com/minh-dng/sdmx)
act as community leaders and moderators.
They take appropriate enforcement action(s),
including issuing corrections or warnings,
or blocking offending individuals
from some or all participation in the project.
(See reference (2) for examples of such actions.)

**All** community members are responsible
to report Code of Conduct violations to community leader(s);
if necessary privately.
Anyone receiving a report is obliged to respond promptly and fairly,
in particular with heed for the privacy and safety of reporters.

## Appendices
### Interpretation

This appendix gives explanations of how the Code of Conduct is interpreted
in particular cases.

- The Code of Conduct does not distinguish ‘contributors’ from other community members.
  Even questions from new users can be regarded as valuable contributions,
  because they help others to:

  - know the project is being used, and by whom;
  - understand those user(s) intended use-cases; and/or
  - notice lacunae or lack of clarity in documentation and other material
    that can be improved.
